Ruleset
  1. A flagpole casts a circular claim of radius 150 and cannot be attacked.
  2. Two claim circles may never intersect, so poles sit at least 300 apart.
  3. A foundation must fall wholly inside one claim circle, clear of other foundations.
  4. Every other building, the vault included, sits on a foundation.
  5. Each team may place any number of flagpoles, but may have only 2 active vaults at a time across all its claims.
  6. Each claim can hold one vault. A vault seals its claim: only its own team may build inside. Everyone else is locked out until it falls.
  7. A pole with no vault in its circle loses 1 of its 10 HP every tick. At zero it falls.
  8. Foundations lose 1 of their 10 HP every tick unless a vault in the circle is burning logs.
  9. Killing the vault's foundation kills the vault, which starts the pole decaying.
  10. With no vault in the claim, whichever team places one takes the claim.
