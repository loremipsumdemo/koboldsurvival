src/mobs.h. A mob whose picture is missing is drawn as a pink square. Each kind
carries its own raw meat and bones (the bones are items 134 to 150, in the same order), and belongs to its own faction
(data/factioninfo.txt) unless a camp's faction has it. The pictures from mob_19.png on are not used yet: a new kind of
mob takes the next free number. A kind with a model (data/model/mob, listed in data/modelinfo.txt) is drawn by it.
