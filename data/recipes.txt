# Crafting Recipes. Whitespace-separated lines; blank lines and lines starting with # are skipped.
#
# recipe <key> <prop> <item> <count> <hours> <gizmos> <item:count,item:count,...>
#   key     its name in saves: never change it, or orders and research that name it are lost
#   prop    the Advanced Crafting prop it is made at (a key of data/props.txt with the advanced flag)
#   item    id of the item it makes (data/itemdb.txt), and count how many one batch makes
#   hours   game hours one batch takes a worker of skill 1 at a prop of speed 1 (higher skill and speed are faster)
#   gizmos  Gizmos a faction spends at a Research Table to unlock it. 0: everyone knows it from the start.
#   then what one batch takes, as item:count pairs with commas and no spaces
#
# Every item with a cooks_into is also a Stove recipe, added by the game: five of it into five cooked, known to everyone.
# The game shows a recipe by the name of the item it makes.
