Emotes: data/emote/emote_<n>.png (no leading zeros), small pictures over a unit's head that show what it is doing
(src/emotes.h). emote_0.png stands in for any emote whose own picture is missing; without it, a pink square is drawn.
A new emote takes the next free number.

Emote	File	Shown when
0	emote_0.png	Stand-in for any emote whose own picture is missing
1	emote_1.png	Mining: working Ore
2	emote_2.png	Woodcutting: working a Tree
3	emote_3.png	Cooking: working a Campfire
4	emote_4.png	Encumbered: carrying 90% or more of the carry limit
5	emote_5.png	Sleeping: lying on a Bed or Bedroll
6	emote_6.png	Hungry: hunger below 1
7	emote_7.png	Lockpicking: picking a lock
8	emote_8.png	Locked in: in a locked occupiable prop (the Prisoner Cage)
9	emote_9.png	Herbalism: gathering at a Mushroom
10	emote_10.png	Unconscious: at 0 health, out cold until it recovers in a bed or bedroll
11	emote_11.png	Dead: a corpse, its Doom reached 100
12	emote_12.png	Playing dead: awake but carried by a hostile, lying still until given an order
13	emote_13.png	Hover cue: over a carried unit whose carrier you have selected, right-click to Put down
14	emote_14.png	Hover cue: over an NPC you can talk to
15	emote_15.png	No upkeep: over a Vault with no upkeep items (logs) in it
16	emote_16.png	Decaying: over a Flag that is wearing down
