# Factions. Tab-separated lines; blank lines and lines starting with # are skipped.
#
# faction <id> <name key> <color> <units>
#   id: a whole number that never changes or gets reused, since saves keep it. 0 is the Player Faction (your
#       units) and must be listed.
#   name key: the language key of its name (FACTION_... in data/language). A key the language files lack is
#       shown as written.
#   color: #rrggbb, for its dot in the Reputation tab
#   units: how many of its units a new level places, together in one camp. 0 for the Player Faction.
#   Then, optional words of key=value, in any order. Those left out take the value in [brackets]:
#     control      player: its units are yours to order. npc: they run themselves. [player for id 0, npc for the rest]
#     factionless  how it takes to the Factionless: friendly (as allies), neutral, hostile (attacks them on sight) [neutral]
#     trespass     an outsider, neither ally nor enemy, standing in its claim: ignore; warn (it is attacked if it stays
#                  on); attack [ignore]
#     downed       what its units do with a hostile unit lying knocked out: leave (it lies); cage (locked in the
#                  faction's nearest free Prisoner Cage, or carried out of its claim when none is free); carry_out
#                  (carried past the edge of its claim and put down); finish_off (killed) [leave]
#     take         what they take from that unit first: nothing; food (what in its pack eases hunger); weapons (food,
#                  and what it holds in its main hand and off hand); everything (its weapons and whole pack, not its
#                  armor). What the taker can't hold is dropped beside it. [nothing]
#     trade        whom it trades with: nobody; allies; not_factionless (anyone not hostile but the Factionless);
#                  anyone (anyone not hostile) [anyone]
#     markup       percent added to what it asks in a trade, 0 to 200 [0]
#     recruit      whom it takes in: never; rescued (an outsider it rescued who wakes up in its bed); factionless (a
#                  Factionless unit who stays a while in its claim); both [never]
#     aid          whom its units rescue and share food with: own (their own faction only; for the Factionless, no
#                  one); allies (allies too); anyone (anyone not hostile) [allies]
#   Your faction's rules can be changed in play, in Faction Rules on the Faction tab, and the save keeps them.
#
# relation <id> <id> <reputation>
#   The starting reputation between two factions, the same both ways, from -100 to 100. Pairs not listed start
#   at 0 (neutral); a faction is always at 100 with itself. At -25 or below their units attack each other on
#   sight; at 50 or above they are allies. Reputations between two other factions never change; the Player
#   Faction's change in play.
