Sound effects: the files in the SFX folder and what plays them.
Each line: the file, the volume slider it follows (Interface sounds or Sound effects), then when it plays.
A missing file is silent. sfx_57.mp3 to sfx_99.mp3 are free: to give a sound its own file, change its number
in src/sounds.h (and here). Working sounds play every 1.6 seconds while a unit works a prop near the camera
(the Forge's every 3 seconds while it burns).
