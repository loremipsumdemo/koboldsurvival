Maps: data/map, packed into data.pak. New Game lists the main map first, then the premade maps, then the worlds
saved in the maps folder next to the game (src/main.cpp, listMaps). Saves refer to a map by its name.

Path	What it is
map/main/	The main map, a world folder: New Game starts on it
map/main/world.txt	Plain text: format, name, created, size in tiles, chunk size, height scale, seabed depth
map/main/look.json	The look: sky, sun, rock and water colors, water motion
map/main/surface.ksrf	The two surface tiles the ground and the sea are drawn with
map/main/r.<rx>.<rz>.kreg	Region files, each 16 x 16 chunks (1024 tiles a side); read a chunk at a time
map/<name>.map	A premade map in the older single-file format: heights, walkability and ground types (src/map.h)
