Music and ambient tracks: loose .mp3 files in the BGM folder next to the game (copied there by the build), not in
data.pak. The music and ambient sliders each loop one track; which plays is set per screen in soundtrackFor
(src/main.cpp). A missing file is silent. The menus opened from the title keep the title's track playing.

File	Slider	Plays
title.mp3	Music	Title screen, and on through New Game, Load Game, Settings and the party menu
bgm_01.mp3	Music	In game
amb_01.mp3	Ambient	In game (the title screen has no ambient track)
