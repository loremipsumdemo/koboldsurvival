Prop models: data/model/model_<n>.glb (no leading zeros), one per kind of prop, numbered from 1 in the order of
src/props.h. model_0.glb stands in for any model that is missing or unreadable; without it, the prop is drawn as a pink
square, and log.txt names the missing file. A new kind of prop takes the next free number.

Unit models: data/model/unit/unit_<nn>.glb (two digits), one per unit Model number (the party menu's Model dropdown). A
body type starts with its own: Feminine unit_01.glb, Masculine unit_02.glb, Androgynous unit_03.glb. A missing one is
drawn as a pink square.

Model	File	Used for
1	unit/unit_01.glb	Model 01 (Feminine by default)
2	unit/unit_02.glb	Model 02 (Masculine by default)
3	unit/unit_03.glb	Model 03 (Androgynous by default)

Mob models: data/model/mob/mob_<nn>.glb (two digits), for the kinds of mob that have one (MobInfo::model in src/mobs.h).
A kind with no model, or whose model is missing, is drawn by its picture (data/mobinfo.txt).

Model	File	Mob
1	mob/mob_01.glb	Gorlicorn (mob kind 18), 2.4 tiles tall
