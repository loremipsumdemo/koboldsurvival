# Update notes
- Four factions, Red, Green, Blue and Yellow, each in its own camp. The Faction Panel's new Reputation tab shows where they all stand.
- Knocking out a faction's units costs reputation with it. Attack one, and others of its faction that see it join in.
- Short Bow, Long Bow and Crossbow, with arrows and bolts.
- Behavior modes: Passive, Block and Sneak. Ranged turns on by itself with a bow or crossbow.
- Right-clicking a hostile unit attacks it.
- Storage props start with things in them, and props are clicked by their actual shape.
- Panels can be unlocked, moved and resized from any edge. Reset All UI Panels is in Settings.
- The world map is a window you can drag. Right-click it to move.

Thanks for playing!
