# Update notes
- New panel layout. Unlock a panel to move it or resize it from any edge, then lock it to keep it. Reset All UI Panels is in Settings.
- The Squad Panel shows three rows of five, with arrow buttons and a draggable scroll bar.
- Gear slots are item cells, like the inventory.
- The world map is a window you can drag. Right-click it to move, and see props and your camera on it.
- The Information Panel has a Time tab, and filters for Athletics and Endurance experience.
- Units at 0 health fall unconscious. Carry them to a bed before their Doom fills, or loot their corpse.
- Shop with traders from the right-click menu.
- The Vagabond scenario: one unit, no gear, no items.

Thanks for playing!
