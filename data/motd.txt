# Update notes
- Plant a flag to claim ground for your faction. Inside your claim you can build foundations and a vault. A vault burns logs as upkeep, and its flag wears away when the vault runs dry.
- Build mode: left-click to place, hold R and move the mouse to turn, and Destroy takes down foundations and buildings.
- New behaviors: Auto-Harvest, Auto-Haul, Eat, Heal, Share, Rest, Leave Bed and Rescue Downed Allies. A behavior that can't run shows why it is waiting.
- Units now have Rested and Comfort needs. Beds and bedrolls can be occupied, and an unconscious unit you carry can be placed in one.
- Mobs roam the world: bears, cows, chickens, boars, wolves, deer, rabbits, fish, crabs, lizards, snakes, pigs, sheep, ducks, turtles, bats, frogs and gorlicorns. They drop meat and bones.
- New scenarios: Pack of Two, Beastmaster and Full Random Camp. Custom Scenario can add mobs to your party.
- Disband sends a unit away for good as an Exile, or a mob back to the wild. Hold the button to confirm.
- Three new unit models, and many more names to pick from.
- Sneaking earns Stealth experience, and carrying earns Strength experience. Angry units calm down after a while.
- New settings: View Range, Show FPS and Show Prop Info.

Thanks for playing!
