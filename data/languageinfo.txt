Languages: data/language/language_<code>.txt, packed into data.pak and also copied next to the game as the
LANGUAGE folder, where an edited file overrides the packed one key by key (src/language.h). Each line is
KEY<TAB>text. English (language_en.txt) is the reference: a key missing from another language shows its English.
The first line, LANGUAGE_NAME, is the name the menus show. English has 760 keys.
